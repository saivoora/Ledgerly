How to Use Ledgerly

A quick walkthrough of the four things that power the dashboard: getting transactions in, the Google Drive Inbox, recurring & subscription detection, and setting up Net Worth.

1

Add transactions manually or via CSV

Manual entry: Go to the tab and click the Add button (top-right). Fill in the date, merchant, amount, type (income or expense), and category, then hit Save. Use the comma-separated Tags field to group related transactions for later filtering.

Bulk import: Open the tab and paste a JSON array of transactions into the text area (use Load sample to see the expected shape), then click Import. For a CSV workflow, export your spreadsheet to JSON or convert each row into an object with the fields date, merchant, amount, type, category and paste the array in. Imported rows are treated the same as manually added ones and can be edited inline afterward.

2

Google Drive Inbox & the daily 8 AM sync

Ledgerly can watch a Google Drive folder as an "Inbox" for statements and receipts. To connect it, open the tab, find the Google Drive Auto-Sync card, and click Connect. Paste in the Drive Folder ID you want to use as the Inbox and pick the sync time (default 08:00).

Once connected, simply drop PDF statements, CSV exports, or receipt images into that Drive folder. Every day at 8:00 AM (your configured sync time) Ledgerly scans the folder for new files, parses them, and turns them into transactions — no manual upload needed. You can adjust the schedule from the cron field in (default 0 8 * * *). The sync status and last-sync time show up on the Import tab.

3

Recurring & subscription suggestions (Keep / Ignore)

Ledgerly scans your transaction history for payments that repeat at regular intervals — weekly, monthly, yearly — and flags them as potential recurring expenses or subscriptions. Each suggestion includes the merchant, amount, detected frequency, and a confidence score.

Open the tab and click Re-scan to (re)run detection. For each surfaced suggestion choose:

  • Keep — confirm it as a tracked recurring expense. Ledgerly remembers the pattern, folds it into your recurring totals, and can auto-categorize future occurrences.
  • Dismiss — mark it as a one-off or noise so it won\'t be surfaced again. Dismissed merchants are excluded from future scans.

Tip: the more transaction history you have, the higher the confidence scores. Re-scan periodically after new imports so newly-appearing subscriptions are caught.

4

Set up Net Worth in Settings

Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities. To turn tracking on, go to and flip the Net Worth Tracker toggle. Two input fields appear: enter your Total Assets (cash, investments, property value) and Total Liabilities (loans, credit balances, mortgage), then click Save Net Worth. Ledgerly computes the value immediately and starts a history chart so you can watch the trend over time.

Fine-tune the view from — set your preferred currency and date format there. Those preferences apply across the dashboard, transactions, and net-worth display.

Everything here is a prototype view — actions like Save, Import, and Re-scan are wired to the UI but persist data once the backend is connected.
